## 09 Warm Ceramic + Teak

- **Where the teak goes.** The cheeks sit in the window's existing 25 px side margins (about 7 mm at the knob's scale), so nothing moves. They stand 1.5 mm proud of the housing, with rounded, oiled edges and straight grain along their length.
- **Housing corners.** They tighten from 26 px to 7 px so the panel meets the wood squarely, as on the Braun.
- **Proportion caveat.** The cheeks are narrow because the margin is narrow. On the Braun they are about a twelfth of the width. Wider cheeks would mean a slightly wider window, about 20–40 px per side. That is a layout change, so it is your call; it is not done here.

## 10 Celadon & Teak (Braun-inspired)

| Element | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Panel | Cooler satin off-white housing, cards and macro panel (E5/D7), closer to the Braun's moulded panel than the warm cream. |
| Keys | Pale celadon (A5). The four modifier keys and M/S sit in one black recessed bank each, like the Braun's PH / AM / SW / FM row. Single keys (EQ, mode, Advanced, ARP) stand on the panel. |
| Preset bar | Panel-coloured, so it does not become a large blue key. |
| Pressed / on | The key sits lower. "On" adds a faint warm face; the icon keeps the accent colour. |

| Kept as is | The approved ivory macro knob, the dark displays, the orange accent. The Braun's red needle is close to the existing accent. |

**Possible next steps for this scheme:**
- celadon-tinted layer knobs;
- a lighter, cooler text grey;
- the Braun's lowercase scale labels.

## 11 / 12 Studio Keys (inspired by the second reference)

- **Keys (A6).** Cream keycaps with soft edges stand about 0.9 mm out of dark wells. The dark gap around each key is part of the look. Pressed sits deeper; on is a warm face.
- **Knobs (K2).** Cylindrical cream caps with a crisp top edge and a black line from the centre to the rim, at each knob's real value.
  - **11:** every knob, including the six macros.
  - **12:** the layer and ADSR knobs only. The approved Blender macro knob stays.
- **What stays the plugin's own.** The value rings, modulation halos and tick dots. Only the cap underneath is new, so the halo clearance is unchanged.
- **Fader.** The same cream as a cap with a black line, in a recessed slot (C5/T2).
- **Not copied.** The reference's silver body and its slide switches. You asked for the buttons and knobs only, so the body stays Warm Ceramic. A silver body would be a quick follow-up.

## First impressions (judge at 100 % on your own display)

- **09** is the safest step: it is everything you liked about 02, made more furniture-like.
- **10** is calm and distinctly Braun. The black key banks give the cards a strong graphic rhythm.
- **11** is the most tactile and "instrument-like". The cream caps make every control the same family; the approved knob's fluting and satin are lost.
- **12** keeps the approved knob as the hero and gives the small knobs a crisper, more legible cap.
