# Recommendations — round 1

These are proposals, not decisions. Nothing is implemented. The choice is yours.

## Proposed overall direction: 06 Hybrid Premium (with three notes)

Your prediction mostly held up in the renders, for concrete reasons (with one caveat, D6's depth):

1. **The macro section can become one object.** A recess (D6) groups the six knobs the
   way the display bezels group the waveforms, with no colour change. **Caveat:** at the
   rendered 0.6 mm it is too subtle (close to D1 at 100 %). Round 2 should test 1.0–1.2 mm
   and a slightly darker or brushed floor.
2. **Active states change form, not only colour.** A2's lit groove and warm face are the
   clearest "on" of every family, and the panel stays quiet when nothing is on.
3. **One metal accent.** The C2 brushed-aluminium cap in a recessed slot is the premium
   object you asked to be adventurous with. It does not cool the GUI.
4. **Light that agrees with the knob.** The lens LEDs and real bevels and bezels now share
   the knob's studio, so the knob no longer looks like the only real thing in the window.
5. **It is still ANDOR/OSP.** It keeps the same warmth, typography and hierarchy, and the
   tabs stay flat.

**Notes for round 2:**
- **Groove weight (A2).** Check the refined groove at 100 % on your display. If it still reads
  as an outline, a 0.8 px groove or a lighter groove floor is the next step.
- **Recess depth (D6).** Deepen to about 1.0–1.2 mm, otherwise the macro section does not read as a section.
- **Brushed floor.** D6 uses a satin floor. A *very* light brushed floor (D2's grain) is the
  natural variant to test if you want the "slightly brushed" note.
