One tonal recording becomes an expressive instrument. This installer puts OSP where your music software looks for it:

  • Audio Unit in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  • VST3 in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • Standalone app in /Applications

Choose Customize on the Installation Type step to leave a format out.

Your music stays as it is

Your presets, samples, packs, custom patterns, preferences and DAW projects are not touched. Projects that use OSP open with this version.

Had an earlier OSP test build?

Earlier test builds were installed in your own ~/Library and ~/Applications folders. If you keep them, your DAW may find two OSPs and load the old one. In Customize, tick Move earlier OSP test builds aside: they are moved (not deleted) to ~/Library/Application Support/ANDOR/Previous Test Builds, where you can put them back.
