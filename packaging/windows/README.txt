ANDOR OSP - test build for Windows (64-bit)

This is a test build: it is not signed, so Windows SmartScreen may say it is from an
unknown publisher. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

What it installs
- The VST3 plug-in: C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\OSP.vst3
- The standalone app: C:\Program Files\ANDOR\OSP (Start menu: ANDOR OSP)
You can choose either or both.

After installing
- Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins (Ableton Live, Reaper, Cubase, Bitwig, FL Studio:
  the VST3 instrument "OSP").
- The standalone: choose your audio device and MIDI input in its Options.

Your presets, samples, packs and patterns live in your Documents folder; installing or
uninstalling never touches them. Sessions saved on a Mac open here, and the other way round.

Uninstall: Windows Settings > Apps > ANDOR OSP (test build), or the Start menu entry.
