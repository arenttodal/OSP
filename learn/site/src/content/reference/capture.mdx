## After a take

A kept take is trimmed (silence before and after removed), stored with the instrument's sounds, and its notes are mapped at once: the layer becomes an instrument of the notes it heard, and NOTES shows them. Replacing a sound this way is one undo step.

OSP keeps about a minute of input history while an input is connected, so GRAB LAST can reach back up to 60 seconds.
