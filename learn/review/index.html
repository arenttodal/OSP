Scenario osp.scene.p1-pedal-loop-pingpong · product commit 6ef3376 · MP4 · WebM · poster

What this showsPedal loop sets what the sustain pedal does to One Shot notes on each layer: Normal (a standard sustain pedal), Forward (a forward loop) or Ping-Pong (a forward and reverse loop), chosen in the playback key's menu.
