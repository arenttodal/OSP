# Custom ARP Patterns — CP2: integration into the existing arpeggiator

19 November 2026, branch `claude/custom-arp`. The mapping decision (the hybrid, as **CONTOUR**)
is recorded in `02-hybrid.md`. CP2 puts it, with variable-duration events, into the one shared
arpeggiator of ANDOR OSP and the ANDOR shell (ANDOR ANALOG). There is no editor yet (CP3), and
patterns are not saved in sessions or presets yet (CP4).

## What was built

| Part | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Pattern | `src/engine/ArpCustomPattern.h` | A fixed-size value with up to 256 events and 128 steps. Each event is a rank or a rest, has a duration in exact ticks (960 per step, at least 30) and an optional velocity. It also holds R and the mapping (CONTOUR / DEGREES, append only). `valid()` enforces every limit. |
| Mapping | `planCustomPool`, `customNote` | Allocation-free ports of the lab's `planHybrid` / `noteFor`. The plan is built once per change of the held notes, OCTAVES or R, never per note. |
| Scheduler | `Arpeggiator` | `Settings::custom` and `setCustomPattern` / `clearCustomPattern`. Each nominal step plays the events whose onsets fall inside it. A subdivision waits in the bounded queue STRUM uses and keeps its place inside the swung step. GATE is a share of each event's own duration. A new pattern takes over at the next step and keeps the phase. |
| Hand-over | `CustomPatternExchange` | One triple buffer per card, from the message thread to the audio thread. There is no lock or allocation, and the reader never sees a half-written pattern. |
| Parameters | `InstrumentHost::addCustomPatternParameters` | One bool per card: `arp.custom`, `arp.cardB.custom` and `arp.cardC.custom`. They are appended last with version hint 36, and the shell adds them after STRUM's (`ProductInfo::customPatternParameterHint`). They are off by default. `StrumReader` reads them alongside STRUM. |
| Products | `PluginProcessor`, `ShellProcessor`, `CardArpLanes` | `InstrumentHost::setCustomArpPattern (card, pattern)` and `customArpPattern (card)`. Card A's pattern goes to the main arpeggiator (and so to every linked lane); B's and C's go to their lanes. The ANDOR shell's AUX follower copies its lane, pattern included. |
| Renderer | `RenderConfig` (`"arp": { "custom": { … } }`) | `research/configs/custom-*.json` covers the spec's corpus (five-step contour in both mappings, repeated subdivisions, sixteenths and thirty-seconds, sustained events with rests, odd length with a split) plus `custom-off-reference.json`. |

## Decisions made in CP2

- **The parameter.** `arp.pattern` stays as it is. Appending a 19th choice would move every
  choice's normalised value and change recorded automation. The audit's recommendation applies:
  a separate CUSTOM bool per card. Which pattern a card plays is state, not a parameter (CP4).
- **CUSTOM on without a pattern** plays the selected style, sample for sample, so a session or
  automation with CUSTOM on can never go silent.
- **STRUM does not apply to a custom pattern.** Version 1 is monophonic; chord events are the
  spec's later extension.
- **When a key is released,** the waiting subdivisions taken from that key (and its octave
  copies) are dropped. Notes already sounding finish their gate. All Notes Off, CUSTOM off, a
  host jump and the transport restarting drop everything waiting, as with STRUM.

## Guarantees and their tests

`[arp-custom]` in `tests/unit/ArpCustomPatternTests.cpp`:

- **The approved mapping, exactly.** The engine's notes equal the lab's `noteFor` (hybrid and
  degree-cycle) over 400 random chords (duplicates, wide voicings, the MIDI top) × 1–4 octaves
  × R 0–9 × every rank × 6 cycles.
- **The lab's rhythm, to the sample.** Every reference gesture plays the lab's `rhythmSequence`
  note for note, within one sample:
  - as Stage A and Stage B, and an edited pattern (split, split, rest);
  - in both mappings, over three chords, without swing and at swing 40 %.
- **The 18 styles are unchanged.** With CUSTOM off, with a pattern loaded or not, and with
  CUSTOM on without a pattern, every style is event-identical. The 18-style golden passes.
- **Rhythm:**
  - a rest plays nothing and does not repeat the last note;
  - a split note retriggers at each half;
  - a merged note triggers once and ends at GATE × its whole duration;
  - ARP RATE scales every onset.
- **Odd length:** a 5.5-step pattern stays in phase over eight cycles while CONTOUR climbs four
  octave positions and comes home.
- **Real time:**
  - block sizes 1, 7, 64, 333 and 1024 give identical output at 44.1, 48 and 96 kHz;
  - no allocation;
  - at most 32 notes wait in a step;
  - nothing hangs.
- **Cancellation:** a release, All Notes Off and CUSTOM switching off drop waiting notes.
- **Swap:** a new pattern takes over at the next step with the phase kept.
- **Display and copy:** the display shows the pattern's notes, and a copied arpeggiator keeps
  its pattern.
- **Validity:** empty patterns, rests only, events too short, ranks above R and more than 128
  steps are all refused.
- **The research configs** parse into their patterns.

`[plugin][arp-custom]` in `tests/plugin/CustomPatternPluginTests.inc`:

- the parameters are off by default, recalled, and an older session opens with them off;
- a pattern hands over per card and reads back; an invalid pattern or none clears it;
- CUSTOM on without a pattern renders the same audio as off;
- with a pattern, the instrument receives three notes per four steps, and the ARP display shows
  them;
- unlinked, card B plays its pattern while A plays UP;
- no allocation on the audio thread while a pattern plays and changes.

The parameter tail test lists `arp.custom` last (version 36). The schema golden gains exactly
the three CUSTOM lines. The shell's order test accepts them after the products' own.

## Not in CP2

- **CP3:** the CUSTOM row in the pattern list, Create Pattern, MIDI recording and import, the
  editor (SPLIT / MERGE / EXTEND / REST, LENGTH).
- **CP4:** patterns saved in sessions and presets (embedded, so a missing external file never
  breaks a preset), the pattern library, and per-card assignment in the interface.
- **CP5:** captured onsets, rests and quantisation from recordings. The lab's Stage B exists;
  the capture does not.
- **CP6:** captured velocity and microtiming. A per-event velocity field is already present and
  played; nothing sets it yet.
- **Host tests in real DAWs, and listening on real recordings.**
