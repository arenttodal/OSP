# Custom ARP Patterns — R2: the interface redesign

20 November 2026, branch `claude/macos-release` (the successor of `claude/custom-arp`). CP3/CP4
(`04-cp3-cp4.md`) made custom patterns possible; R2 makes them easy. Everything musical stays as
it was: no engine change, no format change, no parameter change. This is shared code, so it
ships in ANDOR OSP and every ANDOR shell product (ANDOR ANALOG) at once.

## What changed

| Where | Before (CP3/CP4) | After (R2) |

| Choosing | a nested popup menu (Factory / Your patterns / Edit / Create) | **CustomPatternBrowser**, an anchored panel: ON CARD X (the card's pattern, **Edit**), RECENT (other cards' patterns and ones applied this session that are nowhere else in the list), YOUR PATTERNS (the library), FACTORY PATTERNS (collapsible, remembered), **+ New Pattern**. A click chooses (CUSTOM on, one undo step) and closes. Opening and closing it changes nothing. |

## New interactions

- **Notes from rests.** Double-click a rest: a note there, one step at most, on the rank under
  the pointer; the rest's time around it stays rest. A note is never overwritten, nothing is
  made outside the pattern. A new pattern starts as one eight-step rest to draw on.
- **Edges.** Drag a note's right or left edge (grips on the selected note; a resize cursor):
  its length on a quarter-step grid (Alt: 1/32), the pattern's length unchanged. The time comes
  from, or goes to, the rests next to it, never from a note; every event and every rest left
  keeps at least 1/32 step. One undo step per drag.
- **Event menu** (right-click): Split, Merge with Next, Extend into Rest, Make Rest (keep its
  time) or Make Note, Insert Step After (pattern gets longer), Delete Note / Rest and Its Time
  (pattern gets shorter). A rest made and time removed are separate, labelled actions:
  **Delete** makes a rest and does nothing to a rest; **Shift-Delete** removes the time.
- **Long patterns** (more than 32 steps) scroll sideways (the wheel, the track under the grid);
  the selection is kept in view. Durations are never changed to fit.
- **Help** (?): a short card over the grid.

## Safety

- **Rebuilds ask only when they would lose work.** TIMING and GRID rebuild the pattern from the
  take. When the pattern differs from what the current reading gives (manual edits), the editor
  asks *Rebuild pattern from original take? — Your manual edits will be replaced. You can undo
  this action.* (Cancel / Rebuild). The same choice again, or MAPPING (which never rebuilds),
  asks nothing.
- **Record and Import ask** before replacing a pattern with manual edits (or a pattern with no
  take, such as a factory one).
- **Undo restores whole documents.** The editor's history is `arppattern::SnapshotHistory<Document>`
  (the take, its tempo, the interpretation and the pattern): undoing a rebuild brings back the
  pattern *and* the TIMING / GRID it came from. A name is not an undo step (every state carries
  the new name); a factory pattern saved as yours takes its new identity in every state.
- **Preview / Apply / Save / Cancel.** Preview plays the working pattern on the card and puts
  the card back when switched off or on closing. Apply Pattern keeps it on the card (session and
  presets) and never asks. Save to Library writes `~/Documents/ANDOR/Patterns` and never touches
  the card. Cancel, the × icon, Escape, a click outside and the anchor again ask *Discard changes
  to this pattern?* (Keep Editing / Discard) when the document differs from the one opened and
  from the last one saved; otherwise they close at once.
- **Dismissal, not destructors.** `MiniPanel::dismissRequested()` (default: close) is asked by
  both popover hosts (ANDOR OSP's editor and the shell's) for every close the musician asks for
  (`requestClosePopup`: Escape, the backdrop, `closeAnchoredPopover`). A close the host decides
  (another popover, the window going) does not ask; the editor's destructor still cancels a take
  in progress and restores the card, as before.

## Model additions (pure, tested)

`src/model/ArpPatternModel`:
- `edit::resizeEnd`, `edit::resizeStart`: an edge dragged, length kept, across several rests,
  never over a note, never a sliver.
- `edit::placeNote`: a note inside a rest.
- `SnapshotHistory<State>`: the bounded history, now generic (`History` for patterns is unchanged).

Nothing in `src/engine`, the file format (`.andorpattern` schema 1), the state, the parameters
(`arp.pattern`, `arp.custom`, `arp.cardB|C.custom`) or the factory patterns changed.

## Not done, and why

- **Playback position on the grid:** the arpeggiator does not publish a custom pattern's
  position to the interface; adding it is an engine change, outside R2.
- **Duplicate event** in the event menu: not offered (Split and Insert Step cover it without a
  new model operation).
- **Search in the browser:** not added; the lists are short and grouped.
