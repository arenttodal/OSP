Instant
instrument.

Use LIFE so no two notes play the same. Turn DRIVE for heat and FILTER to close it down. Add MOVEMENT, then ECHO. Dial in SPACE for depth.

C04 Six macros, each with its own panel List and imagery aligned by construction: every card holds its macro's real panel, at one size.

Six knobs.
Each opens up.

LIFE

How differently each note is played. 50 % is natural; click the name for pitch, tone, attack and takes.

DRIVE

Saturation from warmth to breakup: TUBE, TAPE or CRUNCH. At zero it is out of the path.

FILTER

LP24, LP12, HP12, BP12 or TILT, with resonance and its own envelope.

MOVEMENT

How the sound changes over time: DRIFT, TAPE, CHORUS, PULSE or a drawn SHAPER.

ECHO

TAPE, BBD or ASCEND, where every repeat climbs an interval.

SPACE

Room, hall, plate, spring and more, each with its own controls.

C07 A closer look, in tabs Tabs advance by themselves while on screen (5 s); a click holds one. Angled close-ups from the mockup studio.

A closer look.

Capture after the fact.

Each card takes its own input: a mic, an interface, or a DAW track over sidechain. Record up to a minute, or pull the last 10, 20, 30 or 60 seconds from the buffer. Notes are found and mapped on the spot.

One take. A multisample.

Note Atlas splits a played phrase into its notes and maps each to its own key, keeping the real note-to-note character of the performance.

Every key its own grain.

Draw curves across the keyboard for POS, SIZE, DENS, SPREAD, TUNE, SCATTER and PAN, with depth and variation. The texture changes with register.

Any source, a grain cloud.

Per layer: POS, SIZE from 20 to 400 ms, DENS from 4 to 40 grains a second, SPREAD, SCATTER, PAN and three window shapes.

Three arps. Fully independent.

One arpeggiator per sound, each with its own on/off, rate, pattern, octaves, gate and swing, or link all three. 18 patterns plus custom step patterns, STRUM for chord steps, host sync, and SATELLITE to send each layer on its own path through the stereo field.

Questions

What kind of sound works best?

One tonal recording: a sung note, a bowed note, a synth, a memo. A phrase works too; Note Map splits it into notes.

Do I need to map samples or set zones?

No. The sound is playable across the keyboard as soon as it loads.

Can I layer sounds?

Up to three, played as one instrument, each with its own controls.
