Tech specs

Sources
WAV, AIFF, FLAC. Mono or stereo, 44.1 to 96 kHz. Folders sorted into pitches, velocity layers and round robins automatically.
Layers
Up to three sounds, each with its own mode (One Shot or Granular), REIMAGINED, EQ, DAMPEN, SYNTH blend and arpeggiator.
Capture
RECORD up to a minute; GRAB the last 10, 20, 30 or 60 s. Mic or interface (standalone), DAW track via sidechain.
Macros
LIFE, DRIVE, FILTER, MOVEMENT, ECHO, SPACE, each with its own panel.
Arpeggiators
Three, one per sound or linked. 18 patterns, custom steps, STRUM, SATELLITE, host sync.
Formats
AU, VST3 and Standalone. macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) at release. Windows not announced yet.
Interface
Scales 80 to 200 %. Undo and redo for sound changes, not only knobs.
