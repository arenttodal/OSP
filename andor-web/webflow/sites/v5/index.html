Somewhere on your phone is a sound you love. A hum on a walk. One perfect note. Something you played once and never again. ANDOR OSP turns it into an instrument you can play across every key, and it stays yours.

Story 01

The voice memo.

You hum a phrase into your phone on a walk. At home it is already waiting in the Inbox, which watches your Voice Memos, Dropbox or iCloud folder. MAP NOTES splits it into its notes, each on its own key. Add LIFE, hold a chord, and the sustain never loops.

Story 02

The one perfect note.

One bowed note becomes a whole-keyboard instrument. TAPE FRAME gives it a faded-film version of itself. IMPRINT underneath adds a synth body that still sounds like the instrument, and holds after the bow has stopped.
