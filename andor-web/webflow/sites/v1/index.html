What kind of sound works best?

One tonal recording: a sung note, a bowed note, a synth, a memo. A phrase works too: Note Atlas splits it into notes on their own keys.

Do I map samples or set loop points?

No. The root is detected, the sustain continues the recording itself, and the sound is playable before the analysis even finishes.

Will my bounces always sound the same?

Yes. All variation comes from stored seeds, so a project renders identically every time.
