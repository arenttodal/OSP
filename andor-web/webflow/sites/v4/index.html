Workflow

Built for sessions
that matter.

Renders identically

All variation comes from stored seeds. The bounce you approve is the bounce you deliver.

Projects reopen exactly

The sample, its corrections and every setting are stored with the session.

Portable instruments

An .ospinstrument carries recordings, analysis and settings to another computer.

Offline and private

No account, no cloud, no network. Your audio never leaves the machine.

Undo for sound

Undo and redo cover sound changes, not only knob moves.

Fails beautifully

Noise, no pitch, tiny or huge files: it plays as well as it can and tells you why.
