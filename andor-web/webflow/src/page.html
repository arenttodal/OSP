LIFE · the performance engine

Every note is performed,
not replayed.

Repeated notes and chords stop sounding like a machine. LIFE was calibrated on real repeated takes: at 0 % every note is identical, at 50 % it is a believable player.

Shaping and space

Six macros.
Each opens its own world.

  1. 01

    Life

    The performance engine: each note a slightly different take, varied the way a player varies them.

  2. 02

    Drive

    Tube, tape and crunch circuits, level-matched, reacting to how hard they are fed.

  3. 03

    Character

    A filter that moves the source's own body resonances.

  4. 04

    Movement

    Drift, tape wow and flutter, BBD chorus, pulse, and a shaper for tempo-locked or hand-drawn rhythm.

  5. 05

    Echo

    Tape, BBD and ASCEND, which puts a pitch shifter in the loop so the repeats climb an interval.

  6. 06

    Space

    Four reverb engines: ROOMS, ABYSS (huge and freezable), VINTAGE and BLOOM.

Beyond one note

Built for the way you actually record.

Note Atlas

One phrase becomes many samples.

Record a melody once. MAP NOTES finds each note, places it on its own key and keeps the real note-to-note character of the performance. Edit, split, merge, retune and fill the keyboard.

Live Capture

Grab what you just played.

Every card has a REC key. Record a take of up to a minute, or grab the last 10, 20, 30 or 60 seconds after the fact, from a microphone, an interface or a DAW track. It is chopped into notes and mapped for you.

KEY DNA

Every key its own grain.

In Granular mode, KEY DNA gives each key its own grain behaviour: curves you draw across the keyboard, variation and individuality, so a texture changes with where you play.

And everything around it.

01

Modulation

Two LFOs with custom shapes, two envelopes and the mod wheel. Sixteen routes, dragged onto any knob, and modulation of a modulation's depth.

02

Arpeggiator

Eighteen patterns with rate, gate, octaves and swing, synced to your host. Each layer can run its own, or all linked.

03

Synth and Imprint

A warm oscillator under the recording. With IMPRINT, the recording's own spectral body shapes it, so the synth sounds cut from your sound.

04

Granular

Any layer can play as grains, with KEY DNA shaping each key's behaviour across the keyboard.

05

The Library

Every sound you load is kept, searchable and auditionable. An Inbox watches your Voice Memos, Dropbox or iCloud folder.

06

Presets and recall

Projects reopen exactly, and bounces render the same every time. An instrument file carries its recordings and settings to another computer.

07

Expression

MPE with per-note bend of ±48 semitones, pressure and slide, aftertouch, the mod wheel and CC macros. Mono mode with legato and glide.

08

Private by design

Works offline. No account, no cloud: your audio never leaves your machine.

Questions

Asked often.

Does it work with my DAW?

ANDOR OSP is being built as AU, VST3 and Standalone for macOS. Before release it will be validated in Logic Pro, Ableton Live and Reaper, and we will list every host it has been tested in.

Can I use my own sounds?

Yes, that is the point. Drop in any WAV, AIFF or FLAC recording, record from an input, or grab the last seconds you played. A phone memo works as well as a studio recording.

What kind of recording works best?

Anything with a pitch: a voice, a bowed or plucked string, an organ, an old synth, a sustained texture. Unusual sounds still play; if a sound has no clear pitch, OSP asks you for the key instead of refusing it.

How is it different from my DAW's sampler?

A sampler maps and loops what you give it. OSP starts from one recording and makes it an instrument for you: it finds the root, sustains past the end of the recording, performs each note a little differently, and can transform it while it stays recognisably your sound.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything works offline, there is no account, and your audio never leaves your computer.
