## In short

- **ANDOR OSP (the instrument) collects nothing.** It has no account, no analytics, no crash
  reporting and no update check. It makes no connection to the internet.
- **Your sounds stay on your Mac.** Recordings, presets and your Library are stored locally and
  are never sent to us.
- **The website** collects your email address only if you join the waitlist, and only to tell you
  about ANDOR OSP.

## The instrument

ANDOR OSP is a plugin (Audio Unit and VST3) and a standalone app. It works entirely offline.

**What stays on your Mac.** The sounds you load are copied into OSP's own folder so that projects
still open when the originals move. OSP also keeps your Library (sounds, tags, collections,
favourites, notes and history), your presets and the instruments you export. These are stored in
`~/Library/Application Support/OSP` and `~/Documents/OSP`. We have no access to them.

**The microphone.** In the standalone app, OSP asks for permission to use your audio input so that
**REC** (RECORD and GRAB) can turn what you play into an instrument. It listens only while a REC
page is open. Nothing is kept until you keep a take, and nothing leaves your Mac. In a DAW, OSP
hears only what your DAW sends to its sidechain input. You can withdraw the permission at any time
in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone**.

**Inbox folders.** If you choose folders to watch (for example a Voice Memos export, Dropbox or
iCloud Drive), OSP reads new audio files from those folders, and only those. Any syncing is done by
the service you chose, under its own terms, not by OSP.

**Sharing.** An exported `.ospinstrument` file contains your recordings, their analysis and your
settings. It goes wherever you send it. OSP never uploads it.

**Removing everything.** Uninstalling the plugin leaves your Library in place. To delete it too,
remove the two folders above (see [Installation](/installation#uninstall)).

## The website

**Hosting.** This site is hosted by [[hosting provider, e.g. Framer B.V.]]. Like any web server,
it processes technical data needed to deliver pages and keep the site secure: your IP address,
browser type, the page requested and the time. [[Retention period of server logs, per the
provider.]]

**Waitlist.** If you join the waitlist, we store your email address, the date you joined and
[[any other field, e.g. country, or "nothing else"]]. We use it only to tell you about ANDOR OSP:
its release, and [[other topics, e.g. early access]]. The legal basis is your consent, which you
can withdraw at any time with the link in every email or by writing to us. We use
[[mailing provider]] to store the list and send the emails. We keep your address until you
unsubscribe, or [[retention period]] after the last email we send you, whichever comes first.

**Analytics and cookies.** [[Choose one: "The site uses no analytics and sets no cookies other than

**Listening on the site.** The sound comparisons play in your browser. Nothing you play or switch
is recorded or sent to us.

**Emails you send us.** When you write to us, we use your message and address to answer you, and
keep them for [[retention period, e.g. 24 months]] in case you write again. A sound you attach is
used only to look into your question and deleted afterwards.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how
we use it, and to receive it in a portable form. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it
at any time. Write to [[privacy email]]; we answer within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the data protection authority
where you live or to ours, [[supervisory authority, e.g. Datatilsynet]].
