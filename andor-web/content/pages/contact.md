## Help us help you

If something isn't working, please include:

- your Mac model and macOS version;
- your DAW and its version (or "standalone");
- the version of ANDOR OSP (**☰ → About**);
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.

If a particular sound behaves strangely, you can attach it. We use it only to look into your
question, and delete it afterwards.
