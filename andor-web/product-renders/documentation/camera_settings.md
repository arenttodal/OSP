# Cameras (pass 2)

Angles are measured from the face normal: `pitch` tilts toward the instrument's front edge and `yaw` turns
around the normal. All cameras use a 36 mm sensor. All are defined in `andor_scene.py` (`CAMS`).

| Camera | Use | Lens | Pitch / yaw | Distance | f-stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | pass 1's angle, for comparison (neutral) | 75 mm | 27° / −24° | 1.16 m | f/11 |
| **B** | elevated three-quarter: the front fascia and one side show the thickness, and the face stays legible | 85 mm | 54° / −24° | 1.30 m | f/11 |
| hero_A | 9:16 at exactly pass 1's framing (dark), for current vs revised | 75 mm | 27° / −24° | 1.12 m | f/9 |
| hero_B | 9:16 cinematic with camera B (dark) | 85 mm | 54° / −24° | 1.06 m | f/9 |
| macros | the six macro knobs | 85 mm | 50° / −18° | 0.64 m | f/11 |
| display | the B layer's screen | 100 mm | 42° / −20° | 0.30 m | f/8 |
| corner | the right side rail and the front-right corner | 100 mm | 66° / +38° | 0.26 m | f/8 |

The instrument floats 9 cm above the floor in the neutral studio and 28 cm in the dark set (as in pass 1).
It is turned −9° in its plane for A and −11° otherwise.
